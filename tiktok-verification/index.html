Platform Overview

Unified Multi-Account Publishing
Built Specifically for TikTok Creators

CreativeAppsLab PostPilot allows agencies and video creators to connect their TikTok accounts, upload short-form videos once, and dispatch posts simultaneously.

⚡
Single Upload Fan-Out
Upload high-definition 9:16 videos once and dispatch to multiple selected creator profiles at the same time.
🔒
OAuth 2.0 & Token Vault
Tokens are encrypted using AES-256-GCM at rest with automatic background token rotation before expiry.
📊
Queue & Error Isolation
BullMQ background worker isolates transient network issues and provides one-click retries without duplicate posts.
